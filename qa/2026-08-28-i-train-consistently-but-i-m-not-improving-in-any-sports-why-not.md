I train consistently, but I'm not improving in any sports. Why not?

Answer:

You may not be strengthening the primary sport muscles, including those that surround your hips, torso, and shoulders. Start with 2 to 3 sets of a movement that protects you from injury and allows your joints and muscles to work together, such as the glute bridge. Lie on your back with your knees bent 90 degrees and your feet flat. Squeeze your glutes and raise your hips until your body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders. Hold this position for 2 seconds, lower, and repeat for 6 to 10 reps. (To increase the difficulty, hold a knee against your chest as you raise your hips.) Then perform split jumps (leaping into the air from a lunge position and alternating your front leg) for 2 to 3 sets of 3 to 5 reps a side.
