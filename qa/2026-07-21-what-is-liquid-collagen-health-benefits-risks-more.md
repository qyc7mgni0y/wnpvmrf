The Takeaway

Collagen is a protein naturally found in the body that provides strength and elasticity to skin and other tissues. Liquid collagen supplements, as well as those in gummy, powder, and capsule form, are marketed to improve skin, hair, nail, and joint health.

Research on many of the possible benefits of supplemental collagen is not yet definitive. But some limited evidence suggests it may help with skin appearance, joints, weight management, and heart health.
