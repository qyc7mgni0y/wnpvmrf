# Quick Hotel Gym Workout

I'm traveling again this week, so I need to do at least one hotel gym workout, and hopefully tomorrow I will get into a regular gym to deadlift. This morning I worked out in the hotel gym here in NYC (while trying not to get sucked into watching ESPN report on the Brett Favre trade), doing this hotel gym workout as fast as possible...

Supersets1A) Wide Grip Seated Row - 3x81B) 1-Arm DB Standing Shoulder Press 3x8 with 55pound db's

[Hotel Gym Workout](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/)

2A) Narrow Grip Seated Row - 3x82B) DB Chest press - 1x20 with the 55's (That's all the gym had, although its' about 5 pounds heavier than most hotel gyms)

Then I walked down to Battery Park, then back up to Wall Street, across it, and then back to my hotel.

Last night I did an even longer walk...

[Ruth Bader Ginsburg’s Trainer Pays Tribute By Doing Pushups at Her Casket](https://github.com/wrmmfz80fo/kvopwx/blob/main/misc/2026-08-05-ruth-bader-ginsburg-s-trainer-pays-tribute-by-doing-pushups-at-her-cas.md)

I arrived in New York City last night around 5. We flew right over the city and it felt like we were only 10 feet above the skyscrapers. It was strangely intense. I don't usually get to fly over cities like that...

After a light dinner I started walking. That's what I do when I travel. I'll walk 12-17 miles in a day. I've done that in Sydney, Vancouver, Auckland, and Belfast, and I did several miles in Manhattan last night. In my opinion, I'd rather walk by ten buildings than go into one.

So I walked by the World Trade Center, amazed to see not much has changed since 2004, the last time I was at the site (although I appreciate how long it takes to build a foundation having lived beside many condo constructions in Toronto).
