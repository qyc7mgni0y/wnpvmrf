# This Woman Trained Like ‘Tomb Raider’ Star Alicia Vikander for 45 Days

The character of Lara Croft has been through countless incarnations over the last couple of decades, evolving with each new version of the Tomb Raider video game, and famously brought to life by Angelina Jolie in the early 2000s. She was most recently played by Oscar-winner Alicia Vikander in the 2018 big screen reboot of the film franchise, and in a recent video, YouTuber Lexie Limitless takes on the challenge of recreating the intense fitness regime that the actress followed while getting in shape to portray the adventurer.

[This Woman Trained](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/8419714912.html)

[Alicia Vikander for Days](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Lexie reaches out to Magnus Lygdback, the Hollywood trainer who helped Vikander gain 12 pounds of muscle in just three months while preparing for the physically demanding role, and who has also worked with James McAvoy and Ben Affleck to transform their bodies for movies.

Lygdback explains that he and Vikander used a combination of HIIT workouts and strength weights training to hone Lara Croft's lean, strong physique, and he takes Lexie through a demonstration of the different exercises in the program, including an assault bike, lunges, goblet squats, dumbbell flys, and ice skaters.

"I tried to play it cool, but this was by far the most challenging workout of my life," says Lexie. She then uses the principles she learned in her session with Lygdback, along with workout guidance from his app, to continue training for three days at a time, with one rest day in between.

The first three days of workouts are exhausting, and Lexie admits she can "barely make it through this without taking a break" as she isn't used to doing so much hard cardio. She soon finds, however, that biggest and most surprising challenge that arises over the first couple of weeks is eating enough food to sustain the muscle growth she has planned. "It's a complete lifestyle change for me so far, but I'm feeling very empowered to make it through the rest of the training," she says.

[Chisel Out a Six-Pack With Cable Crunches](https://github.com/wrmmfz80fo/kvopwx/blob/main/misc/2026-08-22-chisel-out-a-six-pack-with-cable-crunches.md)

"Working out is a challenge, but I discovered that my nutrition routine is the real key to building muscle," she says. In addition to the grueling fitness plan, Lexie also adopts Vikander's strict Tomb Raider diet, which involves cutting out all dairy, sugar, and processed foods. Each day starts with three eggs and a fist-sized serving of vegetables, followed by 20 grams of protein in a shake and a workout. Her post-workout lunches and dinners would consist of a fist-sized portion of protein, another fist of slow carbs, and two fists of veggies, with one three cheat meals allowed per 20 meals.

As the challenge continues, Lexie finds her endurance in the workouts improves and she is able to complete her cardio without needing to take a break. "I feel like I'm really starting to hit my stride," she says.

[Like Tomb](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1619701377.html)

Lexie takes a DEXA scan before and after the challenge, and is able to conclude that over 45 days, she gained 6.1 pounds of muscle, and lost 4.9 pounds of body fat.

"Gaining over 6 pounds of muscle isn't something that I even realized I was capable of doing, let alone in only 45 days," she says. "Overall I loved this workout and diet plan, it felt like something I can sustain going forward in my life, especially since I was seeing such incredible results. And even though the exercises were hard, I feel so good and healthy. The quarantine funk is definitely out of my system now. I've never felt this good about my body... It was definitely worth the effort."
