# Chiefs Receiver Tyreek Hill Showed Off His Quarantine Workout

You don't get the nickname "Cheetah" without good reason. In a video outlining his lockdown fitness regime, Kansas City Chiefs wide receiver Tyreek Hill demonstrated how he trains to keep up that famous speed.

[Chiefs Receiver Tyreek](https://pepvora.com/rwbptm8h6o/)

Joined by his family for a workout in quarantine, Hill starts off the workout with intense cardio. He challenges his daughter to keep up with him on a series of "tempo runs" (also known as a lactate threshold run). A tempo run is essentially a sprint which pushes the body to its limits, improves the body's ability to use lactate, and can help you run faster.

[Tyreek Hill](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/1862435970.html)

[Off His Quarantine](https://pepvora.com/58v5rxf62w/)

Hill does 10 rounds of this, but starts off by saying he'll only do eight — this, he explains, is a Chiefs thing. "You know, as a receiver, you've got to do extra," he says. "If you've got 10 sprints, if your coach gave you 10 sprints, you've got to do two more. "

[Showed Off His Quarantine](https://therapy.bairibu.com/blog/4858592030.html)

[Off His Quarantine Workout](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/4237652459.html)

He keeps the intensity going when they move indoors for a set of weighted walking lunges, some explosive squat jumps, goblet squats (on a balance board), a 1-minute wall sit, and some jumping jack finishers.

Hill revealed last month that he's been "taking a different approach" to his offseason training this year.

"I'm doing yoga two times a week, so I'm getting into yoga a lot," he said. "I've been trying to open up my hips a little more, open up my glutes and things like that. I have been feeling amazing, been getting out of routes even better. It's going to be crazy."
