WHEN WATCHING AHSOKA, it actually takes a minute to realize just how old the titular character is supposed to be. Fans must first keep in mind that they initially met Ahsoka Tano as a teenager during The Clone Wars (in the 2008 The Clone Wars movie and subsequent TV series)—set between Episode II and Episode III. They must then realize that Ahsoka is set around the same time as The Mandalorian (where Ahsoka made her first live-action appearance); after the fall of the empire in Return of the Jedi. That makes Ahsoka at least 30 or so years older, and most online estimates have her at around 47 years old.

And so while actress Rosario Dawson (44 in real life) does a wonderful job playing the mature, grizzled Ahsoka of the show's present, it makes sense that the show would find a different Ahsoka for depicting that vital Clone Wars period in flashbacks (or, well, whatever exactly is happening in the World Between Worlds with Anakin Skywalker).

[Why Young](https://peply.cc/category/igf0i23ek)

Finding a talented and well-cast performer was a great choice for Ahsoka (and one that more blockbuster projects should consider, rather than overusing bizarre de-aging technologies). In having two different performers play the character, we're able to see (in live-action) the juxtaposition between the raw emotion that young Ahsoka had—and that older Ahsoka has seemingly brushed off, opting for a more stoic persona.

As it turns out, the Young version of Ahsoka is actually played by an actor that audiences have seen plenty of over the last several years—including in several box office smash hits.
