Val Kilmer will join Tom Cruise in the sequel to classic jet-flying bro-drama Top Gun, according to The Wrap. The actor will reprise his role of Tom “Iceman” Kazansky, Cruise's aviation rival and eventual anytime wingman.

Officially titled Top Gun: Maverick, the film — which arrives more than 30 years after the original — will follow Cruise's Pete “Maverick” Mitchell as he mentors a new generation of pilots with the need for speed.

For Kilmer, the return to the airfield would mean more than a feel-good 1980s comeback story. The actor is still recovering from a recent battle with throat cancer and trachea procedure that reportedly "reduced his voice to a rasp and rendered him short of breath."

The Kiss Kiss Bang Bang star underwent chemotherapy to combat the disease, an experience Kilmer claims put his past into perspective.

"I was too serious," he told The Hollywood Reporter, discussing his rockstar-esque rise to fame in the '80s and '90s. "I'd get upset when things like Oscars and recognition failed to come my way."

The movie is set to hit theaters July 12 2019, according to The Wrap.
