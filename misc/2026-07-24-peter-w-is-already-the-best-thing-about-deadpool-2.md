# Peter W. Is Already The Best Thing About 'Deadpool 2'

Deadpool 2 doesn’t hit theaters for another few weeks, but that isn’t stopping people from buying tickets in droves and freaking out over what appears to be the sequel’s best new character, Peter W.

“We are on the edge of our seats to see what Ryan Reynolds and his team have planned for Deadpool 2. With the 24-hour advance ticket record shattered, moviegoers seem just as excited as we are to see our favorite anti-hero back on the big screen.” Ken Thewes, Chief Marketing Officer at Regal, told Deadline.

And sure, people are psyched to see Ryan Reynolds return as Deadpool, but thanks to a bit of genius marketing, Peter W. appears to be the film’s biggest selling point so far.

Fans were introduced to Peter W., who is played by Twitter personality and Catastrophe star Rob Delaney, on Thursday, when the final trailer for Deadpool 2 was released. As the trailer wraps up we see Deadpool interviewing the very dad-like Peter, who admits he has no superpowers. "I just saw the ad," he says.

Deadpool gives him the job, which apparently involves skydiving.

Not only does Peter W. look like a dad, he tweets like one too. The marketing department behind the film quietly started a Peter W. Twitter account several weeks ago and it’s chock-full of regular dude tweets.

[The Best Thing](https://pipeprospect.com/category/5o8qf8alj)

"Wife's always telling me to "get out of my comfort zone!" So, I figure making a twitter and doing this #BeDeadpools2nd thing is a great way," Peter’s first tweet read.

In another, Peter simply tweeted, “It’s hump day.”

[Best Thing](https://pepitverte.com/mhlt67qh70/)

Of course, once the trailer dropped for the film, fans and Twitter users got wise to Peter’s account, which now has more than 25,000 followers. Peter even shared a gif of himself with a personal message for Ryan Reynolds that read, “Thank you, Ryan. During our honeymoon, Susan and I savored your work in The Notebook. Good luck on your incredible journey.”

Don’t worry, everyone, you’ll be able to learn more about Peter when the film premieres in theaters on May 18. But for now, go give Peter the follow that he deserves.
