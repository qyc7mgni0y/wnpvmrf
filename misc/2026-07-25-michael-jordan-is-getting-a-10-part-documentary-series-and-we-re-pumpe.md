# Michael Jordan is Getting a 10-Part Documentary Series and We're Pumped

ESPN Films, quickly becoming the go-to for quality sports documentaries, has announced a partnership with Netflix to co-produce The Last Dance, a 10-part look at basketball icon Michael Jordan and the Chicago Bulls' final 97-98' championship run.

The network released a short teaser, with Jordan himself providing the voice-over.

The Last Dance is directed by Jason Hehir, who also recently helmed the fantastic Andre the Giant documentary for HBO. According to ESPN, the doc was made by looking through "more than 500 hours of never-before-seen footage" from the team's historic run to the title, which saw the NBA's popularity soar along with them.

Jordan reportedly gave his full participation to the series, as did "key figures from the Bulls’ championship teams" and "dozens of other luminaries from basketball and beyond." (Hopefully that does not mean Lavar Ball.)
