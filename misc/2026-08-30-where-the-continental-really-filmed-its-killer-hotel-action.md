The series was primarily filmed in Budapest, Hungary. According to Yahoo, the series shot at Origo Studio, where Blade Runner 2049, Dune, Moon Knight, and The Witcher also filmed.

But scenes involving The Continental hotel itself were filmed in New York. The exterior of the building, the same as in the movies, is The Beaver Building at 1 Wall Street Court. The building is 15 floors and consists of condos and a ground floor sushi restaurant.

The interior is Delmonico's, a fine dining restaurant that's over a century old and located at 56 Beaver Street. The foyer is filmed at Cipriani 25 Broadway, a New York landmark and event venue. Episodes of Boardwalk Empire and Gotham have also filmed there.

Is The Continental a real place?

Sorry, no. You can't stay at the assassin hotel because it consists of various New York locations. But you can visit the filming locations to get a sense of the fictional hotel's makeup.
