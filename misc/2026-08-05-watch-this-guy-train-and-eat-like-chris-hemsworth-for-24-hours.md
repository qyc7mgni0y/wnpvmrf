# Watch This Guy Train and Eat Like Chris Hemsworth for 24 Hours

[Hemsworth for](https://therapy.ketopantrylist.com/blog/3084969312.html)

Fitness YouTuber Jesse James West loves to try the most extreme workouts and diets of famous Hollywood actors. He's already spent a day training and eating like Mark Wahlberg and Michael B. Jordan. For his latest stunt, he's trying out a day in the life of Thor actor Chris Hemsworth. The day will include two workouts and 6 meals and snacks.

After he wakes up, West read that Hemsworth likes to start his day by going over everything he's grateful for. So West spends some time making a list of those things before heading to the kitchen to make breakfast.

His breakfast is large, and includes: raisins, one cup of oatmeal, a banana, four egg whites with one egg, a cup of fat-free milk, orange juice, and a protein shake.

West notes that it's a "ton to have in the morning", but that he is following the diet Hemsworth supposedly ate for the Thor films.

The macros add up to 59g protein, 166g carbs, and 13g fat for a total of 1,017 calories.

"Pretty balanced meal, heavy on the carbs. But hey I don't blame him. He's 6'4," says West.

After taking a walk to grab a coffee, West is back in the kitchen for Meal 2, which consists of: cottage cheese, yogurt, mixed nuts, and some sliced turkey. The macros add up to 75g protein, 48g carbs, and 22g fat for a total of 690 calories.

"Chris Hemsworth, you do have a good diet so far. I feel good, I feel fresh, but it has been a lot of food," says West.

He then heads to the beach for his first workout of the day: surfing.

West has never surfed before, so he gets a lesson before he hits the waves.

Following his surfing experience, he eats a snack of beef jerky. The macros add up to 39g protein, 15g carbs, and 3g fat for a total of 243 calories.

Once he gets home, he's back in the kitchen to make Meal 3, which consists of: 10 oz. of chicken breast, kidney beans, broccoli, brown rice, and milk. The macros add up to 79g protein, 66g carbs, and 8g fat for a total of 652 calories.

After taking some time to digest, West hits the gym for an arms and abs workout modeled after Hemsworth's training.

After the workout it's time to eat again, and he gets back to the kitchen for Meal 4: one glass of fat-free milk, 100 grams of asparagus, 10 oz. of salmon, and 100 grams of quinoa. The macros add up to 88g protein, 33g carbs, and 18g fat for a total of 646 calories.
