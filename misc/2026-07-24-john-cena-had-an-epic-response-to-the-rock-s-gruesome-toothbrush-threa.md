# John Cena Had an Epic Response to The Rock's Gruesome Toothbrush Threat

Jimmy Kimmel hosted Dwayne Johnson and John Cena — literally the two biggest stars in Hollywood — on back to back nights. In classic pro wrestling fashion, things got heated, and asses were mentioned.

First, The Rock told Kimmel Wednesday night that while the two big men's WWE rivalry was "so real," the pair are "best friends" now that they've both broken into the acting game. Still, Johnson had a message to relay: "Just say, 'Listen, DJ always wants to remind you that if he ever acts up, he will knock his teeth so far down his throat that he'll stick a toothbrush up his ass to brush them.'"

Cena, who sat across from Kimmel Wednesday night to promote his surprise comedy hit Blockers, did not take too kindly to The Rock's words.

"Listen up Dwayne Johnson, if that is your real name," he delivered directly into the camera, pro-wrestling style. "Because everybody around the world knows that your first name is T, and your last name is Herock, you just pronounce it all stupid and that just gets me even more pissed off."

Cena continued:

"If you think you're making threats to me, by thinking shoving a toothbrush up my bum is a threat, I got some advice for you pal — and by pal, I totally mean my best friend, I don't mean it as an adversarial term — watch Blockers, and then watch it again. Because if you think shoving a toothbrush up my butt is a threat, you got news coming, pal. There's been a funnel up there. There's been a parking cone. There's more traffic in that region than a Monday on the 405 in both directions."

This was... not the end of things. Cena went on to explain: "Your threat to me is you want to clean my butt? Okay pal, you got the job. But if I was you I'd bring a body suit and latex gloves, because down there it's like a Mississippi corn field in downpour. It is just all mud and vegetation, pal."
