Never Have I Ever will not be returning for a fifth season, and according to the creative time behind the show, the plan was always to end Devi's story after four years.

This was both due to the structure of a series set in a high school where each season is mapped onto a school year, and because the cast were inevitably going to age out of their roles (Darren Barnet was 29 when the show began and is now 32.)

"Four seasons for a high school show felt like it made sense," series creator Mindy Kaling said in 2022. "They can’t be in high school forever. We've seen those shows. Like, you've been in high school for 12 years. What is going on here? Also, the actors get older and it starts looking insane that a 34-year-old is playing a 15-year-old."
